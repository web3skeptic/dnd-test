About

Welcome to our Bluebell Ecstatic Dance — a magical outdoor gathering in the heart of Stanmer Park Nature Reserve. Dance freely among the bluebells as Anna-Lisa and Simona guide you through a flowing musical journey.

The Practical Things
Wear layers — the weather in the park can be unpredictable. Bring water and a snack. We use silent disco headsets so the dance doesn't disturb other park visitors.

More info at www.ecstaticdancebrighton.co.uk
